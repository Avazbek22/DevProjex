## Why

After the five runs of [2026-09-29](2026-09-29.md) the build for release was fixed. One last series
on all eleven tasks of the two newest task sets, with two sessions each, gave the numbers to
publish.

## Setup

- Eleven tasks from the last two runs of 29 September on eight repositories: G1, G2 (gin), J1,
  J2 (gson), R1, R2 (axum), JV1 (jsoup), PY1 (click), CP1 (fmt), CS1 (MediatR), GO1 (chi).
- Two sessions per task, 22 sessions per side, three in parallel. Claude Haiku 4.5.
- Cost: $8.32 for sessions, $1.97 for the judge.
