## Complaints from the judge

- **On DevProjex:** cut declaration bodies that forced another read; docstring examples mixed with
  code in search results.
- **On Repomix:** no path markers in the packed output; line numbers that must be mapped by hand; a
  regex engine that rejected complex patterns.
- **On every build alike:** T1 answers were marked wrong for placing helpers in `_client.py`
  instead of `_utils.py`. The same flag on all three builds pointed at the key rather than at the
  tools, and led to the key audit of 8 November.
