**Builds:** v5.2.2 hardening branch `029270bd`, master (5.2.1) · **Other side:** Repomix 1.18.1 ·
**Agent and judge:** Claude Haiku 4.5 · **Format:** separate anonymous arms, three repeats

The first large run after 5.2. It became the baseline for every November change, and its judge
results were later corrected (see [2026-11-08](2026-11-08.md)).

## Setup

Fourteen tasks, 42 sessions per side, 126 in total, about $21 with the judge:

- eleven tasks from the September series: T1 to T8 on httpx, hono and serilog, G1 on gin, J1 on
  gson, R1 on axum;
- three new ones: N1 reviews uncommitted changes in a CRLF clone of httpx, N2 describes the
  contract of one hono adapter folder, D1 reviews the last commit in hono. These are not the
  September N1 and N2.

Context tokens from here on include cached input, so they are much larger than the September
"new tokens".

## What the judge complained about

On us: cut declaration bodies that forced a read, and docstring examples mixed with code in search
results. On Repomix: no path markers in the pack, line numbers to map by hand, and a regex engine
that rejected complex patterns. The judge also flagged the same T1 "helpers belong in `_utils.py`"
on every side, which pointed at the key, not at the tools. That suspicion turned into the key audit
the next day.
