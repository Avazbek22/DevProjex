# 2026-09-15: declaration body limit set to 1,800 characters

## Build

| Name in this file | Commit | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| v5.2 `1515c4a3` | `1515c4a3` | v5.2 after #411, #412 and #414; body size set at start-up with `--search-body-chars` |

## Why this run was needed

On [2026-09-13](2026-09-13.md) the declaration body in search results removed 19% of calls but
worsened coverage, because it went to the wrong declaration; #406 fixed the choice on 14 September.
The open question was the size of the body: the shipping default was 3,000 characters, and a
proposal based on declaration sizes was to keep it there.

Every earlier attempt to measure the declaration body in search results had been spoiled by a
defect of its own. Three were fixed first:

| PR | Defect |
|---|---|
| #411 | Placing the body pushed the search evidence out of the result |
| #412 | The grading oracle mishandled contradictions and negations |
| #414 | C# conditional compilation hid declarations |

## Setup

- One binary, three arms that differ only in `--search-body-chars`: `off`, `1800`, `3000`.
- Tasks T1 (httpx), T5 (hono), T7 (serilog) and N2, three sessions per task and arm.
- 36 sessions, Claude Haiku 4.5, about $3.90.

## Results

| Metric | Body off | Body 1,800 | Body 3,000 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Turns | 173 | **161** | 179 |
| Cost | $1.27 | **$1.21** | $1.38 |
| Complete answers | 2 of 12 | **5 of 12** | 2 of 12 |
| Required paths missed | 22 of 54 | **14 of 54** | 30 of 54 |
| Answers contradicting the key | 0 | 0 | 1 |

1,800 characters won on every measured row. 3,000, the default at the time, was the worst on
every row and produced the first answer the oracle graded as incorrect.

## Decision

- **Kept:** default body 1,800 characters (#415).
- **Rolled back:** the 3,000-character default.

Two earlier conclusions were withdrawn:

- "The body costs coverage." At 1,800 characters coverage is better than with no body.
- The proposal to raise the limit to 3,000. It was based on declaration sizes (70% of
  declarations fit in 1,800 characters) and on the assumption that a cut body forces a follow-up
  read. The sessions showed that the extra length costs more than the cut. Size statistics did not
  predict agent behaviour; only sessions did.

## Gap found during the run

The navigation queries contained no top-level variables:

| Language | Query file | Missing |
|---|---|---|
| Go | `go/navigation.scm` | `var_declaration`, `const_declaration` |
| Python | `python/navigation.scm` | module-level assignments |

Cobra's `EnablePrefixMatching` and Flask's `SECRET_KEY` could not be shown as declarations or read
by `symbol`, so the agent read whole files to find exactly the flags and defaults that are
searched for most. Go package-level variables were added by 29 September. Python module constants
became declarations in #514 in November; the earlier attempt to relate them in the dependency
engine is the rollback recorded on [2026-09-11](2026-09-11.md).
