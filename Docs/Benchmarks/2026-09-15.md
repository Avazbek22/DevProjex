# 2026-09-15: search body at 1,800 characters

**Build:** v5.2 `1515c4a3` · **Agent:** Claude Haiku 4.5 · **Format:** one binary, three arms by
startup flag

## Why this run was needed

Every earlier attempt to measure the search declaration body had been spoiled by a defect of its
own. Three were fixed first: placing the body pushed the search evidence out of the result (#411), the grading
oracle mishandled negation (#412), and C# conditional compilation hid declarations (#414). Only
then was the body measured cleanly.

## Setup

One binary, three arms through `--search-body-chars off|1800|3000`, so nothing else differs.
Tasks T1, T5, T7 and N2, three repeats: 36 sessions, about $3.9.

## Results

| Arm | Turns | Cost | Complete answers | Required paths missed | Contradictions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| off | 173 | $1.27 | 2/12 | 22/54 | 0 |
| **1,800** | **161** | **$1.21** | **5/12** | **14/54** | 0 |
| 3,000 | 179 | $1.38 | 2/12 | 30/54 | 1 |

1,800 characters won on every measured axis. 3,000, the shipping default at the time, was worst on
every axis, and produced the first answer the oracle graded as incorrect.

## Decision

**Kept:** default body 1,800 characters (#415). **Rolled back:** the 3,000 default.

Two earlier conclusions were overturned:

- "The body costs coverage." At 1,800 coverage is better than with no body at all.
- The recommendation to raise the limit to 3,000. It came from declaration sizes (70% fit in
  1,800) and assumed that a cut body forces a follow-up read. Measured behaviour said the extra bulk
  costs more than the cut. Size statistics did not predict behaviour; only sessions did.

## Gap found on the way

The navigation queries had no top-level variables: Go `var` and `const` and Python module
assignments were not declarations. Cobra's `EnablePrefixMatching` and Flask's `SECRET_KEY` could
not be printed as declarations or read by `symbol`, so the agent read whole files for exactly the
flags and defaults people search for. Go was fixed by 29 September. Python module constants became
declarations only in #514, in November; the earlier attempt to relate them in the dependency engine
is the rollback recorded on [2026-09-11](2026-09-11.md).
