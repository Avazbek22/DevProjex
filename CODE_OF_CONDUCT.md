# Code of Conduct

DevProjex adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its Code of Conduct.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the DevProjex community welcoming,
respectful, and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background,
identity, experience, or personal characteristics.

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other
project spaces, as well as when someone officially represents DevProjex.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of different opinions and levels of experience.
- Give actionable, technically grounded feedback.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and help repair their impact.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Protect private information and security reports.

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory language, unwanted
sexual attention, trolling, or publishing another person's private information
are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to
[avazbekolimov722@gmail.com](mailto:avazbekolimov722@gmail.com). Reports will
be reviewed promptly and fairly, with respect for the reporter's privacy and
safety. Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, issue
warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants
when necessary.

The complete standards and enforcement guidelines are defined by Contributor
Covenant 2.1. Its community-impact guidelines were inspired by
[Mozilla's enforcement ladder](https://github.com/mozilla/diversity).
